Privacy

A quiet website should behave quietly.

This site is designed without advertising, behavioral tracking, or unnecessary third-party scripts.

What this website collects

The static website does not set advertising cookies, create visitor profiles, or run third-party analytics by default. The web server may maintain short-lived operational logs for security, reliability, and abuse prevention.

Contact

When you email QuietWire, your message is handled through our email provider and retained as necessary to respond and maintain business records.

Future services

If we add forms, newsletters, or interactive services, we will identify the provider, purpose, retention, and consent boundary before asking you to use them.

Local-first is not a slogan

Our general design preference is to keep data close to the people and organizations who create it, minimize unnecessary collection, and make external services explicit.
