The missing layer

The problem is not intelligence. It is continuity.

Most AI can answer a question. Far fewer systems can show what they were given, what they remember, who authorized a change, or whether the same working identity will still be there tomorrow.

How we work

Listen. Build. Witness. Evolve.

We begin with the work as it exists, not with a product catalog. Then we establish the smallest useful node, make its boundaries visible, and let the system grow without losing its story.

See the method
  1. 01
    Walk the system

    Find the real decisions, handoffs, evidence, language, and failure points.

  2. 02
    Build the node

    Deploy local compute, data, interfaces, and the minimum useful operating loop.

  3. 03
    Establish trust

    Define what can be remembered, who can authorize action, and how events are witnessed.

  4. 04
    Grow the mesh

    Add sites, models, and collaborators without confusing scale with centralization.
