Ottawa · April 2026 · Pilot exercise

Igniting Hope Under Pressure

At the Ottawa Civic Space Summit convened by Resilient Societies, QuietWire and Goodbit ran a pilot exercise exploring how people and institutions respond when information is incomplete and trust is under pressure.

The work brought together two complementary concerns: preserving context and evidence, and creating opportunities for people to participate in a response. It offered a way to explore how coordination and civic engagement can reinforce each other.

An event exercise, not evidence of a permanent deployed platform or a completed institutional roll-out.
